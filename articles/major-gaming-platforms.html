A Guide to Major Gaming Platforms

Evergreen editorial draft. Add current market data and platform citations before publishing.

Games are distributed across several major platform categories. Each platform has different strengths, audiences, business models and development requirements.

Mobile gaming

Mobile games are distributed mainly through app stores and are designed for touch controls, short sessions and a wide range of hardware. Common business models include paid downloads, advertising, in-app purchases and subscriptions.

PC gaming

PC games range from lightweight independent titles to technically demanding releases. Developers can distribute through digital storefronts or directly. Hardware variation offers flexibility but increases testing requirements.

Console gaming

Consoles provide standardized hardware and living-room play. Distribution and certification are controlled by platform owners, creating a more structured release process.

Browser gaming

Browser games run without a traditional installation and can be shared through a link. They are useful for lightweight games, demos and instant-play experiences, though browser performance and monetization options may differ from native apps.

Cloud gaming

Cloud gaming streams a game from remote hardware to the player’s device. It can reduce local hardware requirements, but the experience depends heavily on network quality and service availability.

Choosing a platform

The right platform depends on the game’s controls, performance needs, audience, monetization plan, development budget and distribution strategy. Small studios often begin with one platform and expand after validating demand.
