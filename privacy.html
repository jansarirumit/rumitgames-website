Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all current and future mobile applications published under the Rumit Games developer account unless a specific application provides its own separate Privacy Policy. It applies to our applications on both Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Rumit Games ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile games collect, use, and protect information when you use our applications.

This policy applies to all mobile games published by Rumit Games, including but not limited to:

  • Bloom Burst
  • Long Jump Champion
  • Javelin Champion
  • Trap Shooting Champion
  • Judgement Kachoful Scorekeeper
  • Future games published by Rumit Games

Information We Collect

Our games do not require user registration or login.

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Payment information

Some games may allow you to enter a local player name for gameplay purposes. This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Advertising

Our games display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising ID
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interactions
  • Ad performance information

On Apple devices, our applications do not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and do not access the device advertising identifier (IDFA) for that purpose. Advertisements shown on these devices are non-personalised.

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

View Google Privacy Policy →

In-App Purchases

Some Rumit Games applications may offer optional in-app purchases. These are processed through Google Play Billing on Android devices, or through Apple In-App Purchase on Apple devices.

Payment information is processed securely by Google or Apple. Rumit Games does not receive or store your payment details.

Analytics

Some applications may use analytics services to understand gameplay performance and improve future updates.

These services may collect anonymous usage information such as:

  • Game starts
  • Game completions
  • Scores
  • Session length
  • Device information
  • Crash reports

No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected.

Local Storage

Some game information may be stored locally on your device, including:

  • High scores
  • Personal bests
  • Game settings
  • Player names entered within the game
  • Unlocks and achievements

This information remains on your device unless you uninstall the application or clear the app data.

Children's Privacy

Rumit Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services, on Android devices
  • Apple App Store services, on Apple devices
  • Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, where applicable

These services have their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
